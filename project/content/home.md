## #how-it-works
### How it works
1. **Match it.** Play a card with the same number or suit as the top card.
2. **Burn 'em.** Hit friends with 2s, 4 chains and Kings that flip the table.
3. **Get out first.** Cards left in your hand are points. Lowest score wins.
